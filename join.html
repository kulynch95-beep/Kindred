About the role

Why Kindred?

Kindred was built to work for caregivers, not just families.

You choose your services, set your availability, and tell us what you're looking for. We use your preferences to bring you opportunities that match.

We have high expectations. Every Kindred caregiver takes pride in their work and understands that we're setting a new standard for childcare in North Florida. In return, we offer competitive pay, new opportunities, and a community that supports you.

Who you are

  • You take incredible pride in what you do
  • Caring for children brings you genuine joy
  • You are patient, calm, and child-focused in every situation
  • You can troubleshoot fussiness and tantrums with love and creativity
  • You encourage learning through play
  • You know when to call for help
Apply

Requirements

  • 3+ years of childcare or related experience (being a mom counts!)
  • CPR & First Aid certified
  • At least 2 verifiable professional references
  • Clean background and driving record
  • Reliable transportation
  • Proof of valid work authorization

What you get

  • W2 and 1099 options
  • Competitive pay
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Cancellation pay protection
  • Choose which services you take on
  • Pick the families you work with

We treat yours like ours.
