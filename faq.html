About Kindred

Kindred & Co. matches on values, not just availability. Many agencies send whoever fits your schedule. We ask detailed questions of both families and nannies, covering childcare philosophy, discipline approach, values, and lifestyle, and we introduce only caregivers who truly fit your household.

Kindred is a white-glove concierge service rather than self-service. You don't post a job or sort through applicants. We hand-select and vet caregivers for your family, so you only meet people we've chosen for you.

The Process

Every Kindred placement follows the same six steps.

  1. Family application. You tell us about your family, your children, your schedule, and what you're looking for in care.
  2. Intake call. We talk through your home, your routines, your priorities, and your dealbreakers. This is where we learn what a great fit means for you.
  3. Curated profiles. We send you profiles of caregivers from our vetted network whom we believe suit your family. You see only the people we've chosen for you.
  4. Interviews. You interview the caregivers whose profiles you liked. We schedule these for you and ask for your feedback afterward.
  5. In-home trials. The caregiver(s) you liked best come to your home for a paid trial shift, so you can see how they work with your children and your routines.
  6. Onboarding. We call references, verify certifications, and run background checks on the prospective nanny.

After you select who you want to interview from the profiles shared with you, we schedule a 30-minute video interview with a candidate. If it goes well, we arrange a paid trial shift — typically 4 to 6 hours — so both you and the caregiver can make sure it is a good fit.

Babysitters can usually be sourced directly from our network and be available immediately, unless you have a special need that requires additional sourcing. Finding a long-term nanny typically takes 1 to 6 weeks between sourcing, vetting, interviewing, trial shifts, background checks, and onboarding.

Timing depends on your schedule, budget, and the specificity of your requirements. Whether you are looking for babysitting or a long-term nanny, we prioritize the right fit over just getting someone to fill a shift.
